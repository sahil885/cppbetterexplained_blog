## Information We Collect

### Analytics Data
We use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use this site. Google Analytics collects anonymous data including pages visited, time spent on pages, browser type, and general geographic location. This data does not identify you personally.

### Email Address
If you subscribe to our email list via the opt-in form on this site, we collect your email address. This is used solely to send you C++ tutorials, tips, and updates. We use **Sender.net** to manage email subscriptions. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email.

### Cookies
This site uses cookies for the following purposes:
- **Google Analytics** — to track anonymous site usage statistics
- **Google AdSense** — to serve relevant advertisements (see below)
- **Preference cookies** — to remember your dark/light mode setting

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
